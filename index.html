GIRO & Buses
Alto dos Caliços Terminal

5 GIRO urban lines (Green, Blue, Red 1 & 2, Orange) link Old Town, The Strip, Marina, Santa Eulália & Ferreiras station. Single ride ~€1.40 / 1-day pass ~€4.

Tourist Offices
3 Albufeira Info Points

Rua 5 de Outubro (by the tunnel, daily), EN 395 Municipal Office (Mon–Sat), and Santa Eulália Office (Mon–Fri).

Parking & Taxis
P1, P5, P6 & Av. da Liberdade

Central taxi rank on south side of P1 near Pau da Bandeira; taxis, radio taxis, and rideshares available around town.
